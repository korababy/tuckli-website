Common questions

Does it work offline? Yes. Your places and items live on your phone; search works with no signal. Recognising a photo needs a connection.

Where do my photos go? They stay on your phone and, when you are signed in, in your household’s private storage. Only your household can see them there. A photo you ask Tuckli to recognise also goes to our server and on to OpenAI, which lists what is in it and deletes it within 30 days (Privacy).
