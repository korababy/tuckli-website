Questions

Good to know.

What can I do with Free?

Set up one home with all its rooms, shelves, freezers and boxes, and type in what is where. Photos that name what they see, Ask and search, and sharing with your household come with Solo, Home and Homes.

What counts as a home?

A home is a top-level place — your flat, a holiday house, a cellar across town. The rooms, shelves, freezers and boxes inside it are free and unlimited.

Does everyone in the household pay?

No. The plan belongs to the household: one person pays, and everyone they invite uses it.

How does the free trial work?

Solo, Home and Homes each start with 14 days free, once per Apple ID. After that the plan renews at its price unless you cancel. If you cancel, your home moves to Free and everything you added stays.

How do I pay or cancel?

Through Apple, with your Apple ID — Tuckli never sees your card. Cancel any time in your iPhone’s Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

I’m not in Switzerland. What does it cost?

The prices here are in Swiss francs. In other countries the App Store shows the price in your own currency.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Move up or down between Solo, Home and Homes in your Apple ID’s subscriptions, or cancel and keep using Free.
