Privacy

What Tuckli stores

The places, containers and items you add, and the photos you take of them. On your phone always; on our server (hosted by Supabase) when you sign in, so your household can share it and you can get it back on a new phone.

Your household

A household is the people who share one set of places and things. Everyone in it sees the same things, and a log of who stored, moved, renamed, removed, lost or found what, and when — written by our server with each change, shown by the name each person gave themselves. You join a household with a six-letter code someone in it made; the code works once, for 24 hours. When you join, the things you kept alone come with you. When you leave, or the owner removes you, the household’s things leave your phone and stay with the others; the log keeps the lines about what you did.

Recognising photos

When you ask Tuckli to recognise a photo, that photo is sent to our server and on to an image-recognition model (OpenAI) to list what is in it. OpenAI does not use the photo to train its models and deletes it within 30 days. You can always skip this and type the items yourself.

Asking

A word typed in Ask searches on your phone. A question or a command (“where is my hammer?”, “move it to the garage”) is sent to our server and on to a language model (OpenAI) together with the list of your places, boxes and item names, so it can answer from them. OpenAI does not use it to train its models and deletes it within 30 days. A move happens only after the app shows it to you.

So that no one account can use up the service for everyone, our server counts how many photos and questions each account sends in a day. It keeps only today’s count, never what was asked, and the count goes when the account does.

What Tuckli does not do

No advertising, no analytics trackers, no selling of data. No third-party software runs inside the app.

Your account

Signing in with Apple, Google or an email address stores that address, an account id and the language your phone runs Tuckli in with us (Supabase), so your household can share its things, you can get them back on a new phone, and our emails to you speak your language.

Payments

Paid plans are bought through Apple, in the app. Apple takes the payment and sends the receipt; we never see your card or bank details. Our server learns only which plan your household has and when it renews, so every phone in the household gets the same plan.

Deleting your account

Settings → Delete account. Today this sends a request to support and is done by hand within a day; before launch it runs by itself from the app. Deleting removes your account, your sign-in and the things you kept alone, with their photos. Things in a household you shared stay with the others, and the log keeps its lines without your name. You can also write to support@tuckli.app.

Your rights

Signing out keeps your places and things on the phone and removes the account from it. You can ask us at any time what we hold about you, have it corrected, or have it deleted, at support@tuckli.app.

This website

tuckli.app is a static site served by GitHub Pages, which sees your address as any web server does. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from a third party: the fonts are served from tuckli.app itself.

The launch list

If you leave your email on tuckli.app, it is kept on our own server in Zurich with the language of the page, and nothing else: no name, no cookie, no tracking. We use it for one email on launch day, 30 November 2026, to say Tuckli is out, and then we delete the list. Until then you can have your email taken off at support@tuckli.app.
