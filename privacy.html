Privacy

Last changed 31 October 2026.

What Tuckli stores

The places, containers and items you add, and the photos you take of them. On your phone always; on our server (hosted by Supabase) when you sign in, so your household can share it and you can get it back on a new phone.

Recognising photos

When you ask Tuckli to recognise a photo, that photo is sent to our server and on to an image-recognition model (OpenAI) to list what is in it. OpenAI does not use the photo to train its models and deletes it within 30 days. You can always skip this and type the items yourself.

Asking

A word typed in Ask searches on your phone. A question or a command (“where is my hammer?”, “move it to the garage”) is sent to our server and on to a language model (OpenAI) together with the list of your places, boxes and item names, so it can answer from them. OpenAI does not use it to train its models and deletes it within 30 days. A move happens only after the app shows it to you.

What Tuckli does not do

No advertising, no analytics trackers, no selling of data. No third-party software runs inside the app.

Your account

Signing in with Apple, Google or an email address stores that address and an account id with us (Supabase), so your household can share its things and you can get them back on a new phone. Paid plans are bought through Apple; we never see your payment details.

Your rights

Delete your account from Settings → Delete account (today this sends a request to support, done by hand within a day), or write to support@tuckli.app. Signing out keeps your places and things on the phone and removes the account from it. You can ask us at any time what we hold about you.

This website

tuckli.app is a static site served by GitHub Pages, which sees your address as any web server does. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from a third party: the fonts are served from tuckli.app itself.
