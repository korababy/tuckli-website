The licence

Tuckli is offered under Apple’s standard licence agreement. This page adds what is particular to Tuckli: the plans, the household and the things you keep in it. Where the two differ, Apple’s agreement applies.

Who offers Tuckli

Tuckli is sold on the App Store by Marysol Longo, Postfach, 8600 Dübendorf 1, Switzerland. Write to support@tuckli.app about anything on this page; a person reads every message.

Plans and payment

Free costs nothing and stays free. Solo, Home and Homes are monthly subscriptions bought in the app through your Apple ID; the App Store shows the price in your own currency before you buy. Each paid plan starts with 14 days free, once per Apple ID. A plan renews every month unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period, in your iPhone’s Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Apple takes the payment and handles refunds (reportaproblem.apple.com). When a plan ends, your home moves to Free and everything you added stays.

Your household

A plan belongs to the household: one payment covers everyone in it, up to the plan’s number of people and homes. Whoever made the household can remove someone from it. Everyone in a household sees its places and things and the log of who did what, so invite only people you would let into those rooms.

What you keep in Tuckli

The places, things and photos you add are yours. We keep them only to run Tuckli for you and your household, as the privacy page describes. Recognising a photo and answering a question give suggestions: nothing is saved until you confirm it, and a suggestion can be wrong. Tuckli helps you remember where things are; it cannot promise they are still there.

Using it fairly

Photograph your own home and things, not other people’s private papers or anything unlawful. Do not try to reach another household’s data, overload the service or copy the app. An account that does may be closed, after we have written to you where we can.

Ending

You can stop at any time: cancel the plan with Apple and delete your account in Settings → Delete account. If Tuckli ever closes, we will tell you in the app first.

Changes

When these terms change, the date at the top changes too. A change that matters to you is announced in the app before it applies.
